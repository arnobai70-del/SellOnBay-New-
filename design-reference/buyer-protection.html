Buyer protection

Buy with confidence.Pay only when it works.

Every website order is held in escrow. The seller is paid only after you check the files, or the 48-hour review ends without a problem.

What's covered

✓The website doesn't match its live demo
✓Files, database or setup guide are missing
✓It doesn't run after following the setup guide
✓The seller doesn't reply to a reported problem
Exact rules: Refund policy

What's not covered

–Changing your mind after you approve
–Problems caused by your own code changes
–Your hosting bills or third-party services
–New features the demo never had (ask for a customization instead)

If something goes wrong

Open a ticket from your dashboard. The seller gets a deadline to fix it, and our support team steps in if you can't agree. Your money stays in escrow the whole time.

Questions

When exactly is the seller paid?

After you click Approve, or 48 hours after delivery if no problem was reported. Payouts go out every Thursday.

Do I pay extra for escrow?

No. It is included in every website order.

What if I paid by bKash?

Enter your TrxID at checkout. We verify it, then the order starts like any other. Refunds go back to the same bKash number.

Who owns the domain?

You do. It is registered in your name, never the seller's.
