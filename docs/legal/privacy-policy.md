**Last updated: 24 October 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data SellOnBay collects, why we collect it, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. ## The short version

- We collect what we need to run your account and your orders, and to keep the marketplace safe.
- We never see or store your card or PayPal login.
- We do not sell personal data and we do not use ad trackers.
- You can download your data or delete your account yourself from the Your data page.

## 1. What we collect

### Account details
Your name, email address and how you sign in. Sellers and admins also set up an authenticator app for two-step sign-in.

### Orders and messages
The template you bought, your domain, the details of the order, the messages you send in an order chat or ask a seller, reviews, disputes and the evidence you add.

### Seller details
For sellers: the name shown on your listings, your payout method and account (for example your PayPal email or bank details), and the verification needed to pay you.

### Payments
Payments are handled by PayPal. We receive the result of the payment (paid, refunded), the amount and a reference. We do not receive or store your card number or PayPal password. For a test card payment on an example product we keep only the card brand and the last four digits.

### Safety and security data
To stop fraud and abuse we keep a hashed (scrambled) form of your connection address and of a random device ID, together with the actions you take (sign up, order, list a site), for 90 days. We keep a record of important clicks, such as accepting an order or downloading a file: the time, your connection address, your browser and the words you saw. This is evidence if there is a dispute or a chargeback.

### Visit statistics
To see how many people visit and which listings and categories they open, we count page opens using a hashed form of your connection address and browser. There is no cookie for this, the address itself is not kept, and the counts are deleted after 90 days. A browser that sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track is not counted.

### Google Analytics (only if you accept)
If you choose **Accept analytics** in the cookie notice, we load Google Analytics 4. It sets its own cookies and sends your page views (the page address, your browser and device type, and an approximate location worked out from your connection address) to Google, so we can see which pages help visitors. It is never loaded before you accept, and never for a browser that sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. We do not use it for advertising. To withdraw, clear this site's data in your browser; the notice then asks again.

### Live chat
The live chat on public pages is run by Tawk.to. It loads from their servers, sets its own cookies so a conversation continues between pages, and what you type (and any name or email you give) goes to Tawk.to.

## 2. Why we use your data (and our legal basis)

| What we do | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Run your account, orders, escrow, delivery and payouts | To provide the marketplace you asked for | Contract |
| Take payments and refunds | To complete the purchase | Contract |
| Keep the site safe: block contact details in chat, review reports, stop fake accounts and chargeback fraud | To protect buyers, sellers and us | Legitimate interest |
| Keep records of orders, payments and downloads | Accounting, tax and legal duties, and evidence in disputes | Legal obligation and legitimate interest |
| Send emails about your account and orders | To tell you what is happening | Contract |
| Count visits (hashed, no cookie) | To understand what people look for and improve the site | Legitimate interest |
| Google Analytics | To see which pages help visitors | Your consent |
| Live chat | To answer your questions | Legitimate interest |

If you are in the EU, UK or a similar region, these are the legal bases we rely on. Where we rely on legitimate interest, you may object (see your rights below).

## 3. Cookies and similar technology

We use only what the site needs to work:

- a sign-in session, so you stay signed in;
- a few choices kept in your browser, for example that you closed the cookie notice or paused an animation;
- a random device ID cookie used to stop abuse (see Safety and security data);
- the cookies set by the live chat (Tawk.to);
- Google Analytics cookies, only if you choose **Accept analytics**.

We do not use advertising cookies. Any optional cookie is loaded only after you accept it. You can delete cookies in your browser settings; the site may then ask you to sign in again.

## 4. Who we share your data with

We share data only with the providers needed to run the service, and only what they need:

- **Payments:** PayPal, to take payments, refunds and, where chosen, pay sellers.
- **Hosting and database:** our hosting provider and Supabase, which store the data and run sign-in.
- **Email:** our email provider, which sends account and order emails.
- **Domains:** a domain registrar, when you buy a domain through us.
- **Live chat:** Tawk.to, as described above.
- **Analytics:** Google, only if you accept analytics.
- **Bot protection:** Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a form is filled in by a person.

Sellers see what they need to deliver your order, such as your domain and the messages in the order chat. We may share data if the law requires it, to respond to a lawful request, or to protect people from fraud and harm. If SellOnBay is ever sold or merged, your data would go with it under the same protections. We do not sell your data.

## 5. Where your data is stored

Our providers may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where required, we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

## 6. How long we keep your data

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| Account and profile | While your account is open |
| Orders, payments and accounting records | The time the law requires (usually several years), without a link to your name after you delete your account |
| Order chat and dispute evidence | While the order and any dispute are open, then as long as needed for legal claims |
| Hashed safety events | 90 days |
| Visit statistics | 90 days |
| Backups | Removed as backups are rotated |

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the California CCPA), you can:

- **see and download** a copy of your data;
- **correct** data that is wrong;
- **delete** your account and personal data;
- **object** to or restrict some uses of your data;
- **withdraw** consent where we rely on it;
- **complain** to your data protection authority.

You can download your data and delete your account yourself on the Your data page (/account/privacy). Deleting removes your name, payout details, developer profile and notifications and locks the sign-in. Payments, orders and accounting records are kept for the time the law requires, without a link to your name. We cannot delete an account while an order, a dispute or a payout is open. For anything else, email support@sellonbay.com and we will answer within 30 days.

## 8. How we protect your data

Data is stored with access rules per account. Sellers and admins sign in with a code from an authenticator app. Connections to the site are encrypted. Files are kept in private storage and are shared only through links that expire. We limit who on our team can see personal data. No system is perfectly secure, so if a breach that affects you happens we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 9. Children

SellOnBay is not for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change matters, we will tell you by email or on the site before it applies. The date at the top shows the latest version.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does SellOnBay sell my data?
No. We never sell personal data and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

### Does SellOnBay store my card number?
No. Payments are handled by PayPal. We never see or store your card or PayPal login.

### How do I delete my account?
Open the Your data page, ask for deletion and confirm. We cannot delete while an order, a dispute or a payout is open.

### Can I download my data?
Yes. The Your data page gives you a copy of the data we hold about you.

### Does SellOnBay track me?
We count visits with a hashed address and browser, with no cookie, and we skip anyone whose browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. Google Analytics loads only if you click Accept analytics. We use no advertising trackers.

### Who can see my messages?
You and the other person in the order chat. Our team can read them when there is a dispute or a safety report.
