## 2. Buying a website template

### How an order works

1. You pick a template, give us your domain (or choose one), and pay.
2. Your payment is held in escrow. The seller is told to start.
3. The seller puts the site live on your domain within the delivery time on the listing (1 to 7 days). An express option (24 hours) is available for an extra fee shown at checkout.
4. You review the live site for 48 hours. You can accept it, ask for a fix, or open a dispute.
5. If you accept, or do nothing for 48 hours, the order is accepted and the seller is paid.

The price you see at checkout is the price you pay. Domains and hosting are optional extras, priced separately, and a domain is registered in your name.

### Payment

Payments are made through our payment provider, PayPal. We never see or store your card or PayPal login. Prices are in US dollars. Taxes, if any apply to you, are shown before you pay.

### Source files and the licence key

Once your payment is verified and in escrow, you can request a signed link to the seller's files for your order. The link expires after 24 hours and you can ask for a new one. Every download is logged with the time, your account, your connection address and the file, because that record protects both sides in a dispute. The files are never public. Each order comes with one licence key.

## 3. Licence: what you may do with a template

When you buy a template you get a licence to use it for your own business or project, on your own domain. The licence type is shown on the listing:

- **Full transfer:** you own it and the seller stops selling it.

You may not resell, share or give the seller's work to someone else to copy or rebuild. The seller keeps ownership unless the licence says otherwise and may sell the same template to other buyers. Taking a delivered site and then asking for a refund so you can use it elsewhere is a breach of these terms.

You can open a dispute if the site is not as described, does not work, contains malware, or breaches copyright. A dispute needs evidence, such as screenshots or a short description of what does not match the listing. The seller can reply and may fix the problem first.

We look at the evidence, the delivery log, the messages in the order chat and the live site. The outcome can be a full refund, a partial refund, or no refund. Accounts with repeated disputes that we reject are reviewed and can be suspended.

### 7-day bug-fix guarantee

Every accepted order has a 7-day guarantee for faults that were in the delivered site. Report the fault in the order and the seller fixes it at no cost.

### Chargebacks

Please talk to us before you ask your bank or PayPal for a chargeback. A lost chargeback can lead to a permanent ban from SellOnBay.
